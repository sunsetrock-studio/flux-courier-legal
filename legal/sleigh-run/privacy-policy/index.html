Privacy Policy

App: Sleigh Run
Publisher: SunsetRock Studio LLC
Bundle ID: com.sunsetrockstudio.SleighRun
Last updated: November 14, 2026

Overview

Sleigh Run is a Christmas Eve flight game for iPhone, published by SunsetRock Studio LLC ("we", "us"), a Connecticut limited liability company. This policy explains what data is used when you play. We do not run an account system, we do not show ads, and we do not collect personal information.

Data We Collect

Sleigh Run does not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or photos. Nothing you do in the game is sent to a server we operate.

  • Game progress (on this device only): Stars, scores, marshmallows, and how far you have flown are stored on your device with Apple's on-device settings storage. We cannot see that data. Deleting the app removes it. The game does not sync progress to our servers and does not have its own iCloud save. A backup you make of the device, including an iCloud device backup, can include that progress. We cannot read those backups.
  • The Full Christmas Eve purchase: Chapters 1 and 2 are free. A one-time $1.99 in-app purchase, "The Full Christmas Eve," unlocks chapters 3, 4, and 5. It is not a subscription. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your Apple ID, your payment card, or your billing address. Apple tells the app only whether that purchase is active on this Apple ID. Restoring the purchase is handled by Apple on your device, from the unlock screen.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store and StoreKit – Used to deliver the app and the $1.99 purchase. See Apple's privacy policy.

Sleigh Run does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools. It does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps.

Children's Privacy

Sleigh Run does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It is not in Apple's Made for Kids category. It does not show ads and does not include third-party tracking.

Data Retention

We do not store a copy of your game or your purchase. Progress stays on your device until you delete the app. A backup of that device can include it, and we cannot read that backup. Apple keeps the purchase record under your Apple ID, under Apple's own policy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy: SunsetRock Studio LLC, Hamden, Connecticut, at support@sunsetrockstudio.com.
