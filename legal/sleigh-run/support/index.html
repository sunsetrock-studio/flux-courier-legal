Sleigh Run Support

App: Sleigh Run
Publisher: SunsetRock Studio LLC
Last updated: November 14, 2026

What the game is

Sleigh Run is a Christmas Eve flight for iPhone and iPad. You steer the sleigh, drop presents down lit chimneys, and try to finish before sunrise. Santa's Route has 30 levels in five chapters. Chapters 1 and 2 are free. There is no account and there are no ads.

The Full Christmas Eve

Chapters 3, 4, and 5 are a one-time $1.99 purchase called The Full Christmas Eve. It is not a subscription. Apple handles the payment. We never receive your Apple ID or your payment card.

To buy it, finish chapter 2 or tap a locked level on the map, then follow the unlock screen.

Restore Purchases

If you already bought The Full Christmas Eve and it is missing on this device, open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchases. Apple checks the Apple ID on this iPhone or iPad. Use the same Apple ID you used to buy it. Restoring does not charge you again.

Saved progress

Stars, marshmallows, and how far you have flown are saved on the device. Deleting the app removes that progress. A normal device backup, or an iCloud backup of this device, can include it. We cannot see those backups, and the game does not have its own iCloud sync.

The $1.99 purchase is kept by Apple on your Apple ID. Restore Purchases brings it back after you reinstall. It does not bring back the stars saved only on the old install.
